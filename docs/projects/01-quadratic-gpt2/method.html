Project 01 · Method

Linear and quadratic low-rank corrections

The adapter is a trainable correction to a frozen GPT-2 projection, not a model trained from scratch.

Notation

For row activation x ∈ R^(1×d), frozen weight W ∈ R^(d×d_out), and frozen bias b, the base projection is:

y_base = xW + b

LoRA and Symmetric use matched trainable shapes:

Δy_LoRA = (α/r)(xA)B
Δy_Symmetric = (α/r)((xU) ⊙ (xU))P

A,U ∈ R^(d×r) and B,P ∈ R^(r×d_out). In the controlled rank study, α=4. The effective scales α/r are 4, 2, 1, and 0.5 at ranks 1, 2, 4, and 8.

Quadratic structure

Δy_j = (α/r) Σ_k P_kj (xu_k)^2 = xQ_jxᵀ
Q_j = (α/r) Σ_k P_kj u_ku_kᵀ

Each Q_j is symmetric and has rank at most r. Projection directions are shared across outputs; signed coefficients in P can make a particular Q_j indefinite. The expansion (ax₁+bx₂)²=a²x₁²+2abx₁x₂+b²x₂² shows how cross terms arise.
