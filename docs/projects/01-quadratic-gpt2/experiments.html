Project 01 · Experiments

Experiments by protocol

The research was conducted in two broad stages. Exploratory studies identified promising questions; controlled campaigns then tested them with matched budgets, fixed placement, repeated seeds, and separate validation and held-out test evaluation.

The historical experiments explain how the research direction emerged, while the controlled campaigns provide the main quantitative evidence used in the conclusions.

Historical exploration

The early experiments were not designed as one uniform benchmark. They explored whether quadratic or interaction-based adapters were trainable, whether placement mattered, and whether distributing a fixed budget across layers changed performance. Their protocols differ, so their measurements remain separate from the controlled rank and downstream studies.

Best recorded validation, final validation at a fixed step, and held-out final test are distinct quantities and are never merged.

Controlled WikiText-2 protocol

The central language-modelling experiments freeze GPT-2 Small and tokenize WikiText-2 into consecutive 128-token blocks for causal cross-entropy training. They adapt only zero-based Transformer block 0 attn.c_proj and use AdamW, learning rate 3×10⁻⁴, gradient accumulation 4, α=4, ranks 1/2/4/8, and seeds 42/123/456.

The 1,000-step screening and 5,000-step confirmation are independent experiments. The confirmation contains 2 methods × 4 ranks × 3 seeds = 24 fresh runs. The held-out test set is evaluated only from the final checkpoint and does not select method, rank, hyperparameters, or checkpoint.

Controlled AG News protocol

The downstream experiment uses the same frozen GPT-2 backbone and controlled adapter placement with a trainable classification head. It uses 4,096 training examples, a fixed 1,000-example stratified validation subset, the original AG News test set, LoRA and Symmetric, seeds 42/123/456, and 500 optimization steps. It tests transfer to classification rather than repeating language modelling.

Why the protocol matters

The controlled comparisons match backbone, placement, rank, trainable parameter count, optimizer, training budget, and seed set. Separate scaling, convergence, and AG News analyses isolate specific questions about effective scale, optimization duration, and task transfer. This design supports a measured interpretation involving parameter budget, rank, scaling, duration, and task rather than attributing every difference to the quadratic operation alone.
