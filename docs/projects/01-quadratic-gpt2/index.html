Research Project 01 · Exploratory controlled study

Symmetric Quadratic Adaptation

Can structured quadratic interactions improve the expressive efficiency of low-rank Transformer adaptation under controlled parameter budgets?

The project keeps GPT-2 frozen and trains small corrections beside selected projections. It compares the linear LoRA baseline with an activation-dependent quadratic branch, then tests rank, placement, training duration, task transfer, scaling, derivatives, and cost.

Measured outcome. On the controlled 5,000-step WikiText-2 study, Symmetric had lower mean validation and test loss at ranks 1, 2, 4, and 8. On controlled AG News classification, LoRA had higher mean accuracy and macro-F1. The evidence is task dependent.

Evidence at a glance

Language modelling

24 fresh 5,000-step runs: 2 methods × 4 ranks × 3 seeds on WikiText-2.

Downstream classification

Six matched AG News runs, reporting held-out accuracy and macro-F1.

Mechanism and cost

Local Jacobian spectra, adapter Hessians, convergence, and matched rank-4 latency/VRAM.
