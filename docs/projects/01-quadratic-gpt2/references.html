Research Project 01 · Context

Related work and references

This project is an independently evaluated, controlled experimental formulation. It makes no claim that quadratic or nonlinear low-rank adaptation is unprecedented.

Foundational context

Transformers

Vaswani et al., Attention Is All You Need (2017), introduced the Transformer architecture used as the broader framework for GPT-2 blocks and attention projections.

GPT-2

Radford et al., Language Models are Unsupervised Multitask Learners (2019), describe GPT-2. This study uses the public Hugging Face gpt2 implementation and pretrained model identifier.

WikiText-2

Merity, Xiong, Bradbury, and Socher, Pointer Sentinel Mixture Models (2016), introduced the WikiText language-modeling corpus family. The experiment uses the raw WikiText-2 configuration supplied through the Datasets library.

Parameter-efficient adaptation

LoRA

Hu et al., LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models (2021), is the principal baseline. It represents a weight update through low-rank factors. The present implementation uses the equivalent activation form Δy = (α/r)(xA)B at a frozen projection. Its controlled comparison deliberately matches LoRA’s rank-dependent trainable count to the Symmetric Quadratic form.

Adapters

Houlsby et al., Parameter-Efficient Transfer Learning for NLP (2019), established a widely used family of small trainable modules inserted into frozen pretrained models. The current work is related in the frozen-backbone goal but tests low-rank corrections at an existing projection rather than a conventional bottleneck adapter block.

Nonlinear and polynomial low-rank adaptation

LoRAN

Li, Song, and Hou, LoRAN: Improved Low-Rank Adaptation by a Non-Linear Transformation (Findings of EMNLP 2024), applies a nonlinear transformation to a low-rank weight update, conventionally described as ΔW=f(BA). It differs from this study’s quadratic dependence on the input activation at a frozen GPT-2 projection.

PERA

Zhang et al., Polynomial Expansion Rank Adaptation (PERA, Findings of ACL 2026, pp. 13287–13303), expands low-rank weight factors polynomially before composition. Its factor-space construction differs from the directly evaluated activation-dependent symmetric form Δy=(α/r)(xU)⊙2P. LoRAN and PERA are related work, not experimentally evaluated baselines here.

Scope of the literature discussion. These references identify direct conceptual neighbors, not a complete systematic review. The site therefore describes the work as exploratory and independently evaluated rather than novel by assertion.
