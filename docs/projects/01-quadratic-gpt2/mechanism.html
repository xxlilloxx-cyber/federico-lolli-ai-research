Project 01 · Mechanism

Derivatives and learned spectra

These analyses describe what the trained adapters do locally. They do not prove why one method performs better.

Jacobians

J_LoRA = (α/r)AB
J_Symmetric(x) = 2(α/r) U diag(xU) P

LoRA has an input-independent adapter Jacobian. Symmetric has an input-dependent local Jacobian and cannot be represented as one constant ΔW. Analytical derivatives were checked against PyTorch autograd.

Second derivatives

For a fixed linear output reduction c, Symmetric has:

H = 2(α/r) U diag(Pc) Uᵀ

The LoRA adapter-only Hessian is exactly zero. Symmetric Hessian Frobenius norms are nonzero in trained checkpoints. Because this Hessian is input independent for the exact quadratic adapter and chosen linear reduction, repeated tokens are not treated as independent Hessian observations. A full-block Hessian experiment was not completed and is not reported as a result.
