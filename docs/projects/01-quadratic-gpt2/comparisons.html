PERA

PERA introduces polynomial expansion in parameter and low-rank-factor space before factor composition. This study instead computes a quadratic function of each incoming activation: x → xU → (xU)⊙(xU) → P. Both investigate higher-order structure in parameter-efficient adaptation, but the nonlinearity occurs in different mathematical objects. PERA was not an experimental baseline in this project.

QuadraNet V2

For one output coordinate, the Symmetric adapter can be written as xQ_jxᵀ, with Q_j=(α/r)U diag(P[:,j])Uᵀ. This is related to a factorized quadratic form. Across a complete multi-output layer, however, all outputs share the directions in U and use signed output-specific coefficients from P. Similarity at the single-output quadratic-form level therefore does not establish complete multi-output architectural equivalence. The study makes no universal novelty claim.
