For one Symmetric output, xᵀU diag(P[:,j])Uᵀx resembles a factorized quadratic form. Across all outputs, the same U directions are shared and P supplies signed output-specific coefficients. The available evidence does not establish complete equivalence to QuadraNet V2. See the comparison sources and repository reports for the detailed qualification.
