What the controlled study found

The final evidence is a 12,288-parameter, 5,000-step, three-seed LoRA versus Symmetric Quadratic comparison. Best recorded validation and final-checkpoint validation are reported separately.

Uncertainty. Values are mean ± sample standard deviation over seeds 42, 123, and 456. Loss and perplexity are lower-is-better.

Held-out test

In the largest matched-placement advantage, concentrated test perplexity decreases from 43.5567 to 40.7165: 6.52%, calculated from unrounded means. This is different from the best absolute test configuration, four-layer LoRA at 37.31 PPL.
