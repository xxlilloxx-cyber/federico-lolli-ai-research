Lower loss is better; higher accuracy and macro-F1 are better. Validation and held-out test results are reported separately.

The independent 1,000-step screen is not concatenated with these 5,000-step trajectories. In the fresh confirmation, rank 1 changes from LoRA-favoured to Symmetric-favoured between the recorded 2,000- and 3,000-step checkpoints. Ranks 2, 4, and 8 favour Symmetric from the recorded 1,000-step point onward, while the gaps at ranks 4 and 8 narrow by step 5,000.

Overall interpretation

Under the controlled WikiText-2 configuration, Symmetric achieves lower final validation and held-out test loss at every tested rank. Under the controlled AG News configuration, LoRA performs better on average. Scaling and convergence results further show that effective scale and optimization duration affect the comparison. Together, the evidence supports a task-dependent alternative rather than a universal replacement for LoRA.
