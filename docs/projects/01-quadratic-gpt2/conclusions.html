Project 01 · Conclusions

What the evidence supports

Explicit quadratic adaptation is trainable and useful in specific controlled language-model settings, while its benefit is neither task independent nor universal.

Measured

  • Symmetric has lower mean final validation and held-out WikiText-2 loss at ranks 1, 2, 4, and 8 in the 5,000-step block-0 protocol.
  • LoRA has higher mean AG News accuracy and macro-F1 under the matched classification protocol.
  • The constant-scale check preserves the seed-42 rank sign pattern but changes its magnitude.
  • The matched rank-4 latency and memory difference is small on the recorded hardware.

Mathematically guaranteed

LoRA is linear at the adapter level and has zero adapter-only Hessian. Symmetric is quadratic, has an input-dependent Jacobian, and has a nonzero second derivative when learned factors make the reduced Hessian nonzero.

Not established

The study does not establish state of the art, statistical significance, transfer to other model families, causal attribution of performance to Hessian magnitude, or mathematical novelty over all prior quadratic networks. Three seeds and two datasets remain a limited evidence base.
