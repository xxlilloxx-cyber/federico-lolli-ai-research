\begin{abstract}
We compare LoRA with an activation-dependent symmetric quadratic adapter at
matched parameter counts in frozen GPT-2. An independent 24-run WikiText-2
confirmation favors Symmetric in mean final validation and held-out loss at
ranks 1, 2, 4, and 8, whereas six controlled AG News runs favor LoRA in mean
accuracy and macro-F1. A single-seed scaling ablation, convergence curves,
local-Jacobian spectra, adapter Hessians, and a matched cost benchmark show that
the result is task and protocol dependent rather than universal.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Parameter-efficient fine-tuning adapts a pretrained model while keeping its
backbone frozen. We ask whether a quadratic function of a projection input can
offer useful capacity at the same parameter count as a LoRA branch. This is an
independently evaluated formulation and controlled experimental study, not a
claim that quadratic low-rank networks are unprecedented.

\section{Method}
For a row vector $x\in\mathbb{R}^{1\times d}$ and frozen projection
$y_{base}=xW+b$, LoRA and Symmetric add
\begin{align}
\Delta y_L &= \frac{\alpha}{r}(xA)B,\\
\Delta y_S &= \frac{\alpha}{r}((xU)\odot(xU))P.
\end{align}
Here $A,U\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times r}$ and
$B,P\in\mathbb{R}^{r\times d_{out}}$. For output $j$,
\begin{equation}
\Delta y_{S,j}=xQ_jx^T,\qquad
Q_j=\frac{\alpha}{r}\sum_kP_{kj}u_ku_k^T,
\end{equation}
so $Q_j$ is symmetric and $\operatorname{rank}(Q_j)\le r$.

\section{Experiments}
Controlled WikiText-2 experiments use frozen GPT-2 Small, 128-token causal
language-model blocks, block-0 \texttt{attn.c\_proj}, ranks 1/2/4/8, and seeds
42/123/456. Twenty-four fresh confirmation runs use 5,000 optimizer steps.
AG News compares the same two methods over six paired runs and reports held-out
accuracy and macro-F1. Full configurations and provenance are supplied with the
repository.

\section{Scaling, derivatives, and cost}
The seed-42 constant-$\alpha/r$ ablation preserves the screening sign pattern
but changes its magnitude. LoRA has constant adapter Jacobian
$J_L=(\alpha/r)AB$ and zero adapter-only Hessian. Symmetric has
\begin{align}
J_S(x)&=2\frac{\alpha}{r}U\operatorname{diag}(xU)P,\\
H_S&=2\frac{\alpha}{r}U\operatorname{diag}(Pc)U^T
\end{align}
for fixed output reduction $c$. Analytical derivatives match autograd. At rank
4 and length 128, forward latency is $12.157\pm0.243$ ms for LoRA and
$12.184\pm0.236$ ms for Symmetric; peak VRAM is 429.05 and 430.31 MiB.

\section{Limitations and conclusion}
The evidence covers GPT-2 Small, WikiText-2, AG News, three main seeds, one
optimizer schedule, and a focused controlled placement. The scaling ablation
has one seed, and no full-block Hessian was completed. Symmetric provides useful
quadratic adaptation capacity in the tested language-model setting, while LoRA
is stronger on the tested downstream classifier. Neither method is universally
superior on this evidence.
