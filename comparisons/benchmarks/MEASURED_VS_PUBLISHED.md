# Measured results versus published context

## Direct measurements in this repository

Only LoRA and Symmetric Quadratic were compared under identical GPT-2,
WikiText-2/AG News, placement, optimizer, seed, and budget protocols. Canonical
tables are under `results/tables/`.

## Published results from other authors

DoRA, MoRA, HiRA, PERA, QuadraNet V2, LoRAN, and Houlsby-adapter results use
different models, datasets, budgets, or metrics. They are **not directly
comparable** and are not copied into a combined ranking. See the primary
sources for their reported values.
