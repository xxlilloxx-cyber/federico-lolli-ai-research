# Figure provenance: final 5,000-step campaign

The long-study files below are generated by
`scripts/figures/generate_public_long_figures.py` from sanitized canonical CSVs
under `results/tables/long-convergence/`. Curves use the arithmetic mean over
seeds 42, 123, and 456; translucent bands and error bars are **sample standard
deviations**, not confidence intervals. No smoothing is applied.

| Asset | Input and filter | Quantity / aggregation |
|---|---|---|
| `validation-concentrated-5000.png` | `long_validation_trajectories.csv`, `geometry=concentrated` | validation loss by step, LoRA and Symmetric Quadratic, mean ± sample SD |
| `validation-two-layer-5000.png` | same, `geometry=two_distributed` | same |
| `validation-four-layer-5000.png` | same, `geometry=four_distributed` | same |
| `delta-loss-5000.png` | `long_validation_trajectories.csv`, each geometry | mean Symmetric validation loss minus mean LoRA validation loss by step |
| `test-loss-5000.png` | `long_final_per_seed.csv` | final-checkpoint held-out test loss, individual points plus mean ± sample SD |
| `test-validation-difference-5000.png` | `long_final_per_seed.csv` | paired `test_loss_final_checkpoint - final_validation_loss`, individual values aggregated as mean ± sample SD |
| `per-seed-final-validation-5000.png` | `long_final_per_seed.csv` | individual final-step validation endpoints, no aggregation |

Endpoint checks are performed against `long_final_aggregate.csv`; the final
curve point must equal `mean_final_validation_loss` for its geometry and
architecture, subject only to floating-point formatting.

## Controlled rank and mechanism figures

Rank/mechanism figures are generated by
`scripts/aggregation/generate_rank_5000_report.py`,
`scripts/aggregation/export_rank_convergence.py`,
`scripts/figures/generate_mechanism_figures.py`, and
`scripts/aggregation/generate_scaling_ablation_report.py`. No smoothing changes
endpoints. The machine-readable complete manifest is `FIGURE_MANIFEST.csv`.
