# Historical GPT-2 experiments

These campaigns established the adapter families, placement hypotheses,
fixed-budget comparisons, and long-convergence protocol before the later
controlled rank factorial. They are intentionally separate from the controlled
screening and confirmation runs because durations, budgets, and insertion
geometries differ.

Private original evidence remains in the ignored directories `results/`,
`results_extended/`, `results_depth/`, `results_multilayer/`,
`results_local_rank/`, and `results_long_convergence_5000/`. The validated
historical master table is
[`results/tables/historical_master_results.csv`](../../results/tables/historical_master_results.csv).
The campaign subdirectories here contain the reports and publication-safe
tables that were previously under `report/`.

Reproduction uses the immutable YAML files under `config/historical/` and the
legacy-required runners under `scripts/training/`. Historical protocols must
not be combined into the fresh controlled learning curves.
